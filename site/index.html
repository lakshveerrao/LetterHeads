How it works

Every letter is alive.

The letter is the body and the glow is the life. A glowing box around a few of them is a family: a word.

They think for themselves.

Each letter has needs, a nature, dreams and memories. Tap one and ask why it is doing what it does. The answer comes from its own life, not a script. Nobody writes the story and nobody controls them.

Their time is your time.

One day in the valley is one calendar day for you, and night falls when it falls where you are. Leave it running and come back later: the Story so far keeps every chapter, from who was born to who found fire and which words formed.

The ground is the real Earth.

The valley is built from real heights and Copernicus Sentinel-2 satellite imagery, with each era's plants restored from pollen records. The rivers, hills and coasts are where they really are, and the place names are real.

300,000 years in three

Two ways to be here

Watch the story, or step into it.

Watch

Nothing to learn.

The camera finds the best stories for you and a caption says what is happening now. Put it on a spare screen and let a civilization grow in the corner of your day.

Take part

Adopt a letter and help it.

Point out ripe fruit, a safe crossing or a friend. It may listen, or it may choose its own way. Adopting is a relationship, not ownership.

Questions

Is it free?
Yes. There are no ads and nothing to buy.
Do I need an account?
No. Your world is saved in your browser on this device. Clearing your browser's site data clears your world.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes. It runs in any modern browser on a phone, tablet or computer. Nothing to install.
Does it keep running when I leave?
The world lives while the page is open, so leave it running in a tab or on a spare screen. It is saved as it goes, and it picks up where it left off when you return.
Is the history accurate?
The ground, rivers, coasts and sea levels come from scientific data. The letters and their lives are the game's own. Dates still awaiting expert review are marked as such.
Is anything tracked?
No. The game collects nothing about you. Fonts load from Google Fonts.
