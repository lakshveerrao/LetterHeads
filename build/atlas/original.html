Letterheads

A living world of letters, happening right now.

The Letterheads are letters that are alive: the letter is the body, the glow is the head. On their own, they find food, make friends, form words, discover fire and build a civilization. No one writes the story, and no one controls them.

Two ways to be here

  • Watch: the camera follows the best stories for you. Nothing to learn.
  • Take part: adopt a letter and help it. It may listen, or choose its own way.

Four things to know

A letter is a person · The glow is its life · A glowing box is a family (a word) · The caption says what is happening now
